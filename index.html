02//THE CRAFTSMAN ONE CHAIR / ONE BARBER

ONE CHAIR. ONE BARBER. COMPLETE FOCUS.

Most shops run on volume: a waiting room, three chairs and a clock that decides how much attention you get. 21 cutz was built the other way round. One chair, one barber, and a calendar that is never double-booked. Nobody drifts in, and nobody is waiting on your seat.

The cut starts before the clippers do: reading bone structure, growth patterns, crown direction and how your hair falls when it is dry. Then it is executed with discipline: measured guard transitions, deliberate weight lines, a razor-clean perimeter. The aim is a cut that grows out the same way every time.
