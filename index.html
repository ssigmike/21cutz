The Barber

One chair. Full attention.

I opened 21 cutz to work the way good barbering deserves: one client at a time, with no queue at the door and no clock pushing the chair. Every appointment starts with a conversation and a careful look at how your hair grows, sits and falls.

Consistency matters more to me than speed. I read the shape of your head, the direction of your crown and the texture of your hair before the clippers come out, then cut for how you actually wear it, weeks after you leave. It's quiet, precise work, and the whole session is yours.
